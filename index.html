About Professify
Real Cal Poly professors and confirmed Fall 2026 instructors, with ratings synced live from PolyRatings' open API. Pick your major or drop in your flowchart, and Professify shows who's teaching each required course — and how they're rated — so you can plan the term around the right professors.
Professor ratings are pulled live from PolyRatings' public API (github.com/Polyratings/polyratings), on a 0–4 scale, and reflect the current data there. Business, Computer Science, and core General Education course instructors are confirmed Fall 2026 sections from Cal Poly Class Search; for other courses the highest-rated real faculty in that department is shown. Some sample reviews and grade data are placeholders. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Cal Poly, RateMyProfessors, or PolyRatings.
