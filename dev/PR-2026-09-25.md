## What's in the build (19:34 + three patch scripts, see `dev/README.md`)

- **Plans A–C.** The Schedule tabs are **My classes · Plans · Planner**.
  - **Plans** shows each plan as a week, with *Edit plan* and *Game plan*. An empty plan shows one button: *Build Plan A*.
  - **Planner** is where a plan is built. *Classes you need* (from your degree ledger, plus search) sits on one side. Point at or tap a class and every section that fits your open time appears on the week as a green block. Click one to add it. Your degree progress sits behind one button underneath.
  - **Registration game plan:** shows the order to register in, the Round 1 16-unit split, a backup section for each class, and *Got it / Waitlisted / Use backup*. It opens from Plans, from Hawk and from the Oct 12–19 banner.
  - **Friends:** a friend's profile gets a Plans tab. Accepted same-school friends only, with a per-plan *Friends can see* switch that is on by default.
  - **"N in plans":** the number of other students with a section in a plan. It is shown only at 3 or more and never includes you.
  - **Hawk:** *Save as / Replace A–C* on Build my term.
